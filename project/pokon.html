Pokon (POKE-on), the Bubble Planet, is the second and largest planet from the BOU-085 Star. Pokon consists of one supercontinent with a humid and urbanized terrain.

  • Popular Cities
    • Sheer
    • Effervescence City
      • Effervescence is the International Capital of Pokon.
  • Popular Modes of Transportation
    • Foot/Self
    • Airships
    • The Needle (Shinkansen-like railway system)
  • Most Abundant Sapient Populations
    • Cloudfolk
      • Cloudfolk are amorphous creatures that share the properties and traits of, well, clouds. They often take a human-like form.
      • A third of all cloudfolk have visual impairment.
    • Noisers
      • Noisers (sometimes spelled Noizers) are lithe bipedal humanoids.
      • They appear as heavily inspired by Earth's scene and visual kei aesthetics.
