What is BOU-085?

BOU-085 is the official name of the interplanetary star system featuring the BOU-085 star and all that orbits it, including three scenic planets and an expansive asteroid belt.

Where can you find us?

We are located in the 8th quadrant of the Ursa Minor Dwarf Galaxy in the Milky Way- specifically Galactic coordinates (20A.4, 8F3.B, B08.5). Earth coordinates will be found on our Earthlings page.

What’s over here?

Our planets, Pokon, Magda, and Seroj, host a flourishing population of 88.9 billion reported inhabitants, also referred to as “bows” or “beaus”, while 100 million inhabitants report to reside on the Great Asteroid Belt. BOU-085 is home to six sapient categories of creature: Noisers, Cloudfolk, Fairies, Pixies, Beastfolk, and Magi.
